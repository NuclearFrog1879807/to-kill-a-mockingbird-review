To kill a mockingbird book review

About the book

To kill a mockingbird was written in 1960 by Harper Lee and set in 1930's Alabama. The main protagonist is Scout who is a young tomboy with an older brother Jem. Their Father is a lawyer and they don't have a mother. The book is centered around a court case falsely accusing a Tom Robinson, a black man, of rape. The story mainly displays that in hard times and corrupt societies there are still good people.

Characters

The main characters consist of:

  • Scout - Main character, real name is Jean Louise Finch.
  • Jem - Scouts brother, real name is Jeremy Finch.
  • Atticus - Scout and Jems' father who is a lawyer.
  • Calpurnia - Maternal figure to Jem and Finch although she is not in a relationship with Atticus.
  • Dill - Scout and Jems' friend who visits in the summer, real name is Charles Baker Haris.
  • Boo Radley - Neighbour who never leaves his house and is seen as an outsider.
  • Bob Ewell - Part of the Ewell familly known as Maycombs disgrace as they are arrogant and don't contribute much.
  • Mayella Ewell - Bobs daughter who accused Tom Robbinson of raping her.
  • Tom Robinson - The "Mockingbird" of the book who is innocent but falsely accused.

Conclusion

In conclusion I think to kill a mockingbird was a great book and I would deffinately recommend it to anyone no matter what their interest although I would say it was a little hard to understand as it used outdated language.
